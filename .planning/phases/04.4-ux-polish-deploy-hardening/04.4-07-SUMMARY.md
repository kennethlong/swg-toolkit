## Decisions Made

- **PATH CONTRACT** (locked, identical in 04.4-12): `confDir` is the caller-resolved `conf/`
  directory; `pushCore3TreOverride` never re-derives it from `serverConfig.path`.
- **RESET-RECORD-CLEAR CONTRACT** (round-2, locked): `resetCore3TreOverride` never touches
  `serverPush.core3.json` — clearing it is exclusively 04.4-12's Reset handler's job, called
  explicitly and separately after reset.
- **Sanitize, don't escape:** `sanitizeToken` replaces every character outside
  `[a-zA-Z0-9_-]` with `_` rather than escaping quotes/backslashes — this eliminates the Lua
  injection surface (T-04.4-12) entirely rather than relying on correct escaping, and
  double-purposes as a filesystem-safe filename sanitizer.
- **Content-tokened filenames, never in-place overwrite:** every push writes a new
  `swgtoolkit_<slug>_<versionToken>.tre` filename; a differently-named prior archive is only
  best-effort unlinked AFTER the new one lands — matches the source-traced danger of overwriting
  a file a running Core3 has indexed at boot.

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Test assertion compared a passthrough Windows path against a backslash-form path**

- **Found during:** Task 3 (first GREEN test run)
- **Issue:** Test (b) asserted `record.trePath` equals `trePathDir` (backslash form from
  `path.join`), but `translateLinuxPathToHost`'s documented passthrough behavior ("already a
  Windows/UNC path passes through unchanged") correctly preserves the forward-slash form written
  into the fixture's `config-local.lua`. The failure was in the test's assertion, not the
  implementation.
- **Fix:** Changed the assertion to `expect(path.resolve(record.trePath)).toBe(path.resolve(trePathDir))`,
  which normalizes slash style before comparing — the underlying passthrough behavior (no forced
  backslash normalization) is intentional per spec and was left unchanged.
- **Files modified:** `packages/renderer/src/services/core3ServerPush.test.ts`
- **Verification:** All 13 tests green; `tsc --noEmit` clean; full renderer suite (310/310) still
  green after the fix.
