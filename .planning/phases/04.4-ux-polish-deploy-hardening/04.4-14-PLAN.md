**Locked undo contract (identical wording in 04.4-01 and 04.4-10 — this spec asserts EXACTLY this, not a re-deploy):** after Undo, the parked PROJECT (studio dir + umbrella folder + every changeset version folder) is restored byte-for-byte and re-appears in the project list.
