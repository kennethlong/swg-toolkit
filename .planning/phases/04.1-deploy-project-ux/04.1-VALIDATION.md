## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** `pnpm --filter @swg/renderer test` + `pnpm -w tsc --noEmit`
- **After every plan wave:** `pnpm test` (full unit) — must be green
- **Before `/gsd:verify-work`:** full unit suite green **+ a real-Electron UAT** of the deploy/onboarding flow (jsdom is insufficient — the executor-integration blind spot; three real-Electron bugs slipped past jsdom in Phase 4)
- **Max feedback latency:** ~30 seconds (unit)
