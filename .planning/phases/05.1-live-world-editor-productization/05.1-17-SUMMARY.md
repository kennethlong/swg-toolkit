## How the design changed

The plan originally used `wsGetNodeCount` as a **force-finish barrier**, exploiting its
`finishLoadNow()` prologue. It worked, but paid essentially the whole remaining parse synchronously —
one frame advances ~40ms of a ~3.1s parse — i.e. a 1-2s client freeze per reload. The provider shipped
`wsIsParsePending` (v28) specifically so we would not have to and asked us to drop it.

The poll is strictly better and **deleted three pieces of complexity** the barrier required: the
fallback-path barrier-skip, the head-of-line blocking of the drain loop, and the path-conditional ack
semantics. Do not reintroduce the barrier; `wsGetNodeCount` force-finishes, `wsGetGeneration` bumps on
load/unload only, and `getLoadingPercent` returns an ambiguous `0`.

## Scope this plan does NOT cover

**Reload is a trustworthy TIMING signal, not evidence an interior edit took.** After the provider's
`unload()` guard (`0b2e9259c`), a building with server-owned occupants is KEPT across a reload and the
kept root collides with the re-parsed node, so it renders its **pre-edit state** until a zone change or
relog. Plans 12 and 15 therefore carry `verification_instrument_changed` blocks pointing at
per-building interior refresh, with unoccupied-building verification as the interim. This plan's poll
does not and cannot fix that — atomicity and correctness are separable.
