**What v33 is.** The provider root-caused our `findCellAtWorldPosition`-after-`game::loadScene`
defect to their own `engine_gameLoadScene` shim: it never destroyed the outgoing `GroundScene`, so
`ClientWorld::install()` ran twice over a live world, `WorldSnapshot::load`'s same-scene re-stream
found every node already existing, nothing repopulated, and the POB was absent from the sphere tree
— hence the world-cell fallback. Fixed by destroying the outgoing scene first.

**Three consequences for this checkpoint:**

1. **The pointer hazard (§2) is already closed on our side — verified, no action needed.** v33 makes
   cached `Object*`/`CellProperty*` genuinely deleted across a `loadScene` where they previously
   just went stale-but-readable. Our agent already calls `invalidateSceneCachedPointers()` at frame 1
   (BEFORE `cleanupScene`) and again after `loadScene`, clearing `g_capFocus`/`g_latchedFocus`/
   `g_lastRayObj`/`g_probeHoverPtr` and disarming any in-flight edit (`overlay.cpp:1429-1442`, call
   sites `:1487` and `:1506`). That was 05.1-16's own checkpoint finding; it happens to be exactly
   what v33 now requires.
2. **No agent rebuild is needed.** Catalog strings are unchanged (160 rows) and `resolve()` binds
   **by name**; the `ENGINE_HOOKPOINTS_VERSION` 32-vs-33 difference is a **soft warning only**
   (`resolve.cpp:52-55`), not a refusal. Deliberately NOT bumping our constant to 33 right now — it
   would force an agent-DLL rebuild immediately before a live checkpoint for nothing but a cleaner
   log line. Follow-up, not a blocker.
3. **The fix's own behaviour is UNVERIFIED by the provider** (their §6, stated plainly). They ran
   build gates and a boot smoke; the acceptance test is unrun.

**Therefore: run v33's 4-step acceptance test FIRST, before the 13-step sign-off.** It is cheap and

## Part A — running order (the plan does not state this, and order matters)

**Do the server-connected steps FIRST, and the editor-scene step LAST.**

Step 5 loads the offline editor scene. The provider's open defect —
`findCellAtWorldPosition` returns the **world cell** after `game::loadScene` — means any ADD
performed after step 5 derives `cellName: "world"` and writes it to the `.ilf`. That would look
exactly like a `getCellName` bug and is not one.

Recommended order: **1 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 → 10 → 11 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 12 → 13**
(2-4 are side-by-side sketch diffs and can happen any time the app is up; 5 goes last, or relog
after it before doing anything cell-name dependent.)

**Verification instrument (steps 1 and 8).** The plan text says "reload the scene." That is
superseded — use `refreshInteriorLayout` on the occupied cantina (`1082874`):

```
powershell -File packages/harness/scripts/find-live-mapping.ps1
powershell -File packages/harness/scripts/drive-host-command.ps1 -Name '<mapping>' -Action refreshinterior -Id '1082874'
```

Two constraints that replace the old occupied-building ban: refresh does **not** compose with a live
preview (two visible copies), and never call it while armed. And the false-pass trap survives the
fix — an occupied building keeps its live in-session objects across a reload, so unsaved work can
*look* persisted. Trust the bytes: a placement grows **both** `.ilf` files by one `NODE` and leaves
`.ws` **unchanged** (84 bytes of `.ws` growth = the duplicate-node defect is back).
